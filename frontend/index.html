Rwanda e-Procurement (UMUCYO) Integration

MedTender continuously monitors Rwanda's Umucyo portal and direct hospital boards. Compliance checks respect robots.txt and national procurement transparency guidelines.

Suitability & Product Matching Algorithm

Suitability scores are calculated using a multi-factor weighting:

  • 40% Keyword & Classification Match: Matches tender lot descriptions with your Product Catalogue specifications.
  • 30% Technical Capability: Verified ISO 13485 certifications and authorization requirements.
  • 30% Commercial Viability: Risk assessment based on bid security, payment terms, and deadline urgency.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Endpoints and actions are strictly partitioned across 7 roles: Admin, Management, Sales, Biomedical Engineer, Procurement, Finance, and Viewer.
