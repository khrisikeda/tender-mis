MedTender Company Relevance & Match Guide

How Company Relevance Score is Calculated

Unlike simple keyword matching, MedTender computes a multi-factor score:

  • 35% Technical Specification Match: Parameter-by-parameter evaluation against your catalogue (Display, accuracy, battery, interfaces).
  • 30% Portfolio Lot Coverage: Ratio of lots/items in the tender that your company can fulfill.
  • 20% Eligibility & Regulatory Fit: Valid ISO 13485, Rwanda FDA wholesale license, and tax clearance.
  • 15% OEM Brand Authorization: Authorized distributor certificates on file for requested manufacturer.

Compliance Status Badges

  • Compliant: Supplied product meets or exceeds tender specifications.
  • Verification Required: Ambiguous requirement or requires checking special accessories with manufacturer.
  • Partially Compliant: Minor parameter variation or requires optional module.
  • Non-Compliant: Critical specification gap.
  • Unknown: Spec not stated in tender document; inquiry recommended.
